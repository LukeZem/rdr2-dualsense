NAME:     DualSense for RDR2 - Adaptive Triggers and Haptics
VERSION:  1.2.0
CATEGORY: Gameplay  (or: Utilities)

Adaptive triggers for every weapon - each gun with its own pull, firing exactly on the break - and haptics

--- DESCRIPTION ---

[size=5][b]DualSense for RDR2[/b][/size]

[b]All-in-one DualSense support for RDR2: adaptive triggers for every weapon and haptics for everything Arthur feels.[/b]
It also drives the lightbar, the player LEDs and the controller's speaker, shows PlayStation buttons in the game, and
adds its settings to the game's own menu.

[size=4][b]Triggers[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Every weapon has its own pull - take-up, wall, crisp break - and the shot lands exactly on the break
[*]Aimed shots with one gun: hammer / lever / bolt / pump worked with R2; from the hip and with two guns the game cycles them for you
[*]The kick comes with the game's real shot; a clean gun works like clockwork, a dirty one drags like mud;
exhaustion and cold make the pull shaky
[*]Dead Eye: every painted target ticks, every shot of the volley kicks as it fires
[*]Fishing: R2 tugs when a fish bites; L2 carries the real line tension and strains before it snaps
[*]Weapon wheel: a light tick for every weapon you move over; L2/R2 click like a selector switch - never like a shot
[/list]

[size=4][b]Haptics[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Hoofbeats from the real hooves at every gait, on the real ground material (mud, gravel, wood, grass, snow...)
[*]Arthur's footsteps from his real feet, landings, ledge grabs, doors, drawing and holstering
[*]Hits from their direction, nearby gunfire, heartbeat, shivers
[*]Honor going up or down, bites / sips / puffs, gun cleaning, brushing the horse, skinning, herbs, bathing
[*]Five finger fillet, poker, blackjack, dominoes
[*]Gun shots built from each gun's own recordings - the game's sounds, or your sound mod's if you use one
[/list]

[size=4][b]Dialogue haptics (experimental)[/b][/size]
Feel the voices: every spoken line in your hands - the speaker's own voice, its rhythm and tone, from the side they
stand on. In cutscenes and in conversations out in the world. Off by default: turn on [b]Dialogue Haptics[/b] on the
DualSense settings page. If you feel a line before you hear it, raise [b]TV / Headphone Delay[/b] (Auto already knows
Bluetooth headphones).

[size=4][b]Lightbar, LEDs, speaker[/b][/size]
Muzzle flash, hits, Dead Eye glow, lantern flicker, wanted, low health, honor; player LEDs show the rounds
left; the game's own holster and lasso sounds from the controller speaker (off by default).

[size=4][b]Settings in the game's own menu[/b][/size]
Pause > Settings > Controls > Controller > [b]DualSense[/b] - a real page of the game's Settings with every
setting of the mod, changed with left / right like the game's own options. Changes apply when you close the menu.

[size=4][b]PlayStation buttons[/b][/size]
The game's prompts and controller picture show PlayStation buttons instead of Xbox ones - Rockstar's own art, no extra mod needed.

[size=4][b]Microphone (beta)[/b][/size]
Off by default and still in testing: whistle into the controller to call your horse, plus a few voice
commands. Turn it on under Settings > Controls > Controller > DualSense.

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://www.dev-c.com/rdr2/scripthookrdr2/]Script Hook RDR2[/url] with an ASI loader (dinput8.dll or version.dll)
[*]DualSense on a USB cable (Bluetooth: triggers, lightbar, rumble only)
[*]DSX / DS4Windows closed, Steam Input OFF for RDR2
[*]Story mode only
[*]For the settings page and PlayStation buttons: Lenny's Mod Loader (LML, from rdr2mods.com); any game language (page texts are
English) - without LML, settings are in DualSenseRDR2.ini
[*]For the voice commands (beta): Windows' English (US) speech recognition (add it under Settings > Time & language >
Speech on a non-English Windows); the whistle works without it
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
Manual install: copy everything from the zip into the game folder - the one with RDR2.exe in it, not a
subfolder: DualSenseRDR2.asi, the DualSenseRDR2 folder and the lml folder (merges into Lenny's Mod Loader).
Remove other DualSense mods. Settings are in the game's menu (see above) and in DualSenseRDR2.ini (created on
first start, F9 reloads it in game). To update, copy the new files over the old ones - your ini is kept.

[size=4][b]Red Dead Online[/b][/size]
Story mode only. Before going Online, move the mod out of the game folder together with Script Hook, the ASI
loader and LML (as for any mod), and back for story mode. The mod changes none of the game's own files.

--- CHANGELOG 1.2.0 (the file upload's changelog field, one line each) ---
Snow: no more constant vibration; steps and hooves in deep snow are felt
Hoofbeats only from the real hooves - nothing generated - steadier at a gallop; every step of both feet is felt
Pause menu and loading screens: all vibration stops
Water and mud: wading drags harder the deeper and faster you go, with a splash going in; every ground has its own feel
Bushes: a rustle as you walk or ride through them
Explosions: felt from where and how far they go off, with the real blast; close ones shake the triggers
Rain on by default (30%); in fights, steps, hooves and rain step back so shots and hits come through
New defaults: Sounds from the Controller Off, Game's Own Rumble 15%, Rain 30% - applied unless you had changed them
Hold PS for a second to reload the settings (like F9); whistling while aiming no longer moves the gun
